# BUG-026 — runway stamp written despite declined/skipped sections, suppressing the next run's forced re-check

**Logged:** 2026-08-06 · **Source:** GitHub issue #28 (https://github.com/RockyHong/super-bootstrap/issues/28), surfaced during a v2.24.1 consumer sync
**Problem:** `.claude/super-bootstrap-runway.json` is written unconditionally at Phase 2c, including when a pipeline-owned section was skipped or its drift declined. Phase 1 keys `version_stale` off a value match, so the next sync reads a current stamp, skips the forced full enumeration (§ 2b's "every pipeline-owned section gets an actual read-and-compare row"), and the unresolved section never re-surfaces — the stamp records "synced at this version" with no record of coverage. The 2c gate is within-run only (report deleted at close-out), so a covered-then-declined section passes legitimately and the stamp erases the outstanding signal. Observed: consumer repo v2.24.1 — `f4bb41c` wrote the stamp without touching `.claude/rules/index.md`; the repo carried the pre-`paths:` rules-index (eager always-on + duplicate catalog) while stamped current; caught only by a hand enumeration.
**Area:** `plugins/super-bootstrap/skills/harness-bootstrap/SKILL.md` (§ Version-staleness signal, § 2b Version-stale enforcement, § 2c stamp write)
**Prior:** issue offers three shapes — stamp coverage alongside version (re-arm on non-empty pending list) / withhold stamp on incomplete coverage / persist declines as a durable project-owned marker (the mechanism § 2b already gestures at without implementing). Related theme: #27→DEBT-048 (drift machinery blind to its own drift).
