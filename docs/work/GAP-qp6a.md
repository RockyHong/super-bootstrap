# GAP-qp6a — A next step gated on a future date has no contract field and is still offered for resume

**Logged:** 2026-11-26 · **Source:** mailbox notes from `jowagoko` (tip, 2026-11-25, its proposal 2) and `claude-config-manager` (handoff, 2026-11-25, its finding 1, against 7.1.3)
**Problem:** The runway's card contract has no field for a next step that waits on a fixed date, and `session-continue` offers a carry for resume before its date arrives. (a) jowagoko runs a local extension — a card whose next step waits on a fixed date carries `**Due:** YYYY-MM-DD`, read by its `scripts/due_today.py` at SessionStart (declared in its `docs/extensions.md`) — and proposes carrying that field into the runway's `docs/work/README.md` contract so every repo has it. (b) In claude-config-manager on 2026-11-26, `session-continue` offered a carry whose Next step read "compare … after about a week; the reminder … is due 2026-12-02"; the user rejected the ask ("時間還沒到就不要問"). (scope per the two notes, unconfirmed)
**Area:** `plugins/super-bootstrap/skills/harness-bootstrap/assets/` (the shipped `docs/work/README.md`), `plugins/super-bootstrap/skills/session-continue/SKILL.md`; possibly `needs-me`
**Prior:** ccm's proposal — when a carry's next step, or its card's latest Progress, names a due date later than today, hold that carry out of the resume offer, name it with its due date, and offer it once the date arrives.

## Verdict — surface · 2026-11-26

### Findings

- premise age: clean — `git log --since=2026-11-25` over `harness-bootstrap/assets/`, `session-continue/`, `needs-me/` returns no commit.
- root cause (gap confirmed against current code): (b) `plugins/super-bootstrap/skills/session-continue/SKILL.md:34-36` builds the offer from pointer resolution and carry age alone — "Every live carry is offered, a single carry included" (`:32`); no step reads a date out of a carry's Next step or the card's latest `## Progress`, so a carry whose Next step reads "due 2026-12-02" is a live carry and is offered. (a) `assets/work-readme-skeleton.md` § Thread contract and `assets/scale/card-fact-fields.md` (the only optional-field list, `Blast:` alone) carry no date field; a repo-local one is already admitted through the `**Extensions:**` line (`work-readme-skeleton.md:29`), which is exactly how jowagoko runs `**Due:**` today.
- card correction: jowagoko's field sits in the card **origin block** (the Extensions line scopes extensions to the origin), but the origin is "frozen at capture" (`work-readme-skeleton.md:27`). A wait-until date that arises mid-thread — the ccm case, a "compare after about a week" step written at park time — cannot land in a frozen origin. A core carrier for the date has to sit on an appended block (`## Progress` / `## Design`) and/or the carry's Next step slot (`session-close/SKILL.md:23`), not the origin.
- adjacent mechanisms already in the runway: `docs/parked.md` admits "actionable-but-waits-on-a-named-trigger" with `**Fires on:**` (a date is a valid trigger), and capture routing (`card-fact-fields.md`) sends "nameable but waits on a trigger" there — but both are scale-module, capture-time routes for a whole item, not a mid-flight next step on a live card. `docs/work/README.md` already ships a wait marker by party (`waiting on {party}` in a Design block, read by needs-me and autorun); there is none by date.
- overlap: `GAP-pp1p` (carry-ledger lifecycle) edits the same `session-continue` offer step; distinct aim (stale carries vs not-yet-due carries), not a duplicate — but the two edits to `:34-36` should sequence on one diff or the second rebases on the first. No `docs/decisions.md` row covers due dates, carry gating, or a core date field.
- scope reach: `session-continue/SKILL.md` § 2 (offer gather + offer + Output); `session-close/SKILL.md` § Ledger convention slots (if the carry gains a date slot); `assets/work-readme-skeleton.md` § Thread contract + dogfood `docs/work/README.md` (prose-lane mirror); possibly `needs-me/SKILL.md` § Scan and `autorun` § Pick (they read the same wait markers); `log` if a capture-time field is added; `docs/terminology.md` if a term is registered. Cross-skill contract change shipped to every consumer repo.
- attempted: static read of the four skill/asset surfaces + parked/outward headers; stopped at the fork below — the gap is real, the shape of the fix is a contract call.

### Decision needed

- Where does a "not before {date}" wait live in the shipped contract, and who reads it?
- options:
  - A — **Carry-only gate.** session-close's Next step slot (or a new `Due:` slot) states the date; session-continue holds a carry dated after today out of the offer and names it with its date. No card-contract change. Fixes (b) only; a card's own Progress date stays unread, and needs-me still lists it.
  - B — **Block-level wait marker in the card contract, read by the pickup skills.** Mirror `waiting on {party}`: a `## Progress` / `## Design` block names `not before YYYY-MM-DD` (one fixed shape); session-continue holds a carry whose card's latest such block is future-dated, needs-me lists it as not-yet-due rather than actionable, autorun leaves it alone. Covers (a) and (b), works mid-thread, no origin edit.
  - C — **Core origin field `**Due:** YYYY-MM-DD`** (jowagoko's proposal promoted from extension to contract). Covers capture-time dates only; a date set after capture cannot be written (origin frozen) without an Amendment convention, and needs a reader in every consumer that lacks jowagoko's SessionStart script.
  - D — **No core change.** Date-gated work routes to `docs/parked.md` `Fires on: {date}` (scale module) or stays a repo extension; session-continue only learns to skip a carry whose card is parked/date-gated. Leaves non-scale repos with no route.
- recommendation: B — it reuses the existing block-level wait idiom (`waiting on {party}`) the pickup skills already read, survives the frozen-origin rule, and fixes both observations with one marker; A as the minimal slice if the author wants (b) only now.
- settles by: preference / product authority — a shipped thread-contract and cross-skill read change for every consumer repo (no decisions row or spec rules it). After the ruling the build is `full` (behavior-shaping skill prose → RED micro-test per `skill-authoring.md`, plus the skeleton ↔ dogfood README mirror), sequenced with `GAP-pp1p`'s edit to the same offer step.

## Design — 2026-11-26

Option B, settled on the user's delegation ("直接開始做", 2026-11-26 — pick by the verdict's recommendation). The card contract gains a block-level date wait beside `waiting on {party}`: a `## Progress` or `## Design` block names `not before YYYY-MM-DD` (one fixed shape). Readers: session-continue holds a carry out of the resume offer when its own Next step or its card's latest such block names a date after today, and names it with that date; needs-me lists the card as not yet due, not actionable; autorun leaves it alone. No origin field (the origin stays frozen). Ship in `assets/work-readme-skeleton.md` § Thread contract and mirror into this repo's `docs/work/README.md`. Sequenced with `GAP-pp1p` on the same session-continue § 2 diff, and with `DEBT-z0qz` on the same contract file.
