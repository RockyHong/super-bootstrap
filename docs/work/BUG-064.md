**Logged:** 2026-09-20 · **Source:** residual surfaced by the cold triage that refuted `BUG-063` (GitHub issue [#70](https://github.com/RockyHong/super-bootstrap/issues/70))
**Problem:** The seeded skeleton asserts two things a cold consumer reads against each other. § The envelope: "Red and verify are structurally empty — no ceremony — on a diff with no test/runtime surface (docs-only)". Not a re-walk of the `harness-grounding` closed fork ([`decisions.md`](../decisions.md)): the skeleton already ships a cluster-7 row asserting a post-edit obligation, so making its own two lines non-contradictory is internal coherence, not sb taking ownership of consumer authoring discipline — and the fix names no skill.

**Prior:** Suspected shape (not settled) — the structurally-empty clause needs its harness-file exception stated in discipline terms, or row 7 needs to say what a verify pass *is* for a runtime-less diff.
