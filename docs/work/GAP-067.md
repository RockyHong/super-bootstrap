# GAP-067 — The mover axis rides an optional card field, so author-moved work leaks into the drainable count and the board mixes the author's queue with the repo's

**Logged:** 2026-08-28 · **Source:** consumer pilot board after the DEBT-088 migration (3 of 5 "drainable" rows were the author's own portal / sitting work; issue #56 filed for the same leak)
**Problem:** `GAP-064` shipped the actor axis as `Actor: author | external` — an **optional** origin-block field ("absent = derive at pickup") — plus `docs/outward.md` for the (a) sub-shape only. Two consequences. (1) **Silent leak:** a card logged without the field derives as the repo's; `Test-feel: manual` walls only the verify phase, so a Plan whose every step runs on a vendor portal or in the author's editor renders `Start execute` → **T** → drainable. No check fires on the omission. (2) **Mixed folder:** the (b) sub-shape (taste sitting, line-by-line review) stays in `docs/work/` beside repo cards, so the author reads BUG/DEBT/GAP threads to find their own queue — the concern `GAP-064`'s 2026-08-26 Amendment named ("a stage that fixes the drainable count but leaves the author's queue unreadable has served one actor only"). A per-card judgment migration (`DEBT-088` on the pilot) re-homed only the cards its origin listed; three same-day cards missed the list and the field alike.
**Area:** `shared/classify-actionable.md` (§a Actor override, §c Owning card), `skills/todo/assets/render-board.py` + `bench/todo-board/`, `agents/todo.md` § Lane split, `skills/log/SKILL.md` step 2, `skills/drain/assets/eligibility.md`, `skills/harness-bootstrap/assets/scale/{card-fact-fields,outward-skeleton}.md`, dogfood `docs/work/README.md` fact-fields block + `docs/outward.md` header
**Prior:** State belongs in file presence, not in a field buried in the file (`pipeline-design` § State = file presence). Make the mover the **container**: `docs/work/` holds only what the repo moves next; every item the author's or an outside party's hands move next lives in `docs/outward.md`, no field to omit. Capture asks the mover first, before BUG/DEBT/GAP. An open outward entry's `Owning card:` walls that card. The board splits outward by `Waiting on` — your move vs waiting on others.

**Test-feel:** unit (`bench/todo-board/run.sh` goldens + cold probes on the log step) · **Blast:** repo

## Design — 2026-08-28 · settled with user

Ruled on the pilot's board: the clean shape, not another field.

1. **Mover = container.** `docs/work/` admits only cards whose next step is the repo's. An item whose next step is the author's hands or an outside party's — taste sitting, line-by-line review, portal registration, deck, a reply to wait for — is an `OUT-###` entry in `docs/outward.md`. The `Actor:` field is retired from `card-fact-fields.md`; the skeleton, the log door, the classifier and the renderer stop reading it. A card still carrying the field classifies by its thread state and the renderer names it on the `# note:` stderr channel — a leftover is visible, never silent.
2. **Capture asks the mover first.** `log` step 2's first gate: *whose hands move the next step — the repo, you, or an outside party?* The repo → the existing gates. You or an outside party → an outward entry when the container exists (tail asked in one line when missing; no tail drops it), else a card whose Problem line names `waiting on {party}` (the wait override reads it). Mixed → split at capture: the repo's remainder is the card, the other step the entry.
3. **`Owning card:` walls the card.** An open outward entry naming an open card holds that card out of the board body (the hard-block path: `pending unblock` in the footer) and out of the drainable count until the entry closes; the entry's `unblocks` stays the leverage signal. An entry that merely touches a card cites it in `Repo tail` instead.
4. **Board splits outward by `Waiting on`.** `Waiting on` naming `author` → group **Outward — your move**; any other party → group **Outward — waiting on others**. Decide / device / harness / probe groups unchanged.
