# DEBT-z0qz — The card Thread contract routes still-true content to docs only at Resolve, so long-lived cards become parallel docs

**Logged:** 2026-11-26 · **Source:** mailbox note from `jowagoko` (handoff, 2026-11-25), against its runway receipt 5.9.0 and plugin cache 7.1.2
**Problem:** The shipped `docs/work/README.md` § Thread contract and § Capture routing send content that stays true to its owning doc only at Resolve, and a Design revision appends a new block while the old one stays on the chain. A card that never resolves keeps every ruling and every superseded version, and becomes a parallel doc. In jowagoko, 9 of 13 open cards held durable content in their threads; one card had 23 `## Design` blocks and 429 lines; the sponsorship card was the only home for each brand's status; cards in a waiting state stayed in `docs/work/` with "(park)" Progress blocks instead of moving to `parked.md` / `outward/`. (scope per the jowagoko note, unconfirmed)

**Prior:** Sender's proposal, shape open — route at append time: canonical content (still true after the card is gone) goes to its owning pillar now and the card keeps a link; history goes to decisions / git; temp stays on the card. A Design ruling that binds work outside the card lives in its pillar, and a revision edits the pillar. A card exists only while the repo has a nameable next move now; a "waiting" state moves to outward / parked at write time. Recurring measurement is a routine, not a card. ## Verdict — surface · 2026-11-26

### Findings

- premise age: clean — `git log --since=2026-11-25` on both Area paths returns no commits.
- root cause (need-check, confirmed against current code): the contract has no append-time route for canonical content. The only route out of a thread is at the end of its life: `plugins/super-bootstrap/skills/harness-bootstrap/assets/work-readme-skeleton.md:46` (**Resolve:** "Before the delete, a behavior or limitation the card leaves true lands in its owning doc"), mirrored in `assets/harness.md:95` (**Card resolution:**) and `assets/rules-dimension-discipline-skeleton.md:54`. Revisions keep everything on the chain: `work-readme-skeleton.md:35` (Design: "Revision = new Design block that takes over; old stays in the chain") and `:36` (Plan, the same clause). No line asks "does this ruling bind work outside the card?" when a Design block is appended. So a card that never resolves keeps its durable rulings only on the card. The mechanism the card names holds.

It also falls back to "a card whose `Problem:` line names `waiting on {party}`" when `outward/` is absent. A card that starts to wait mid-life has no route out by design. The proposal's "a waiting state moves to outward / parked at write time" reverses a shipped rule. It does not fill a hole.

- options:
