# GAP-h3cz — placed doc rules' `paths:` do not follow a repo's declared doc surface

**Logged:** 2026-11-09 · **Source:** GAP-ha7m's Design block (option iii, split out)
**Problem:** Once a repo declares extra doc-surface globs in `.claude/doc-surface` (GAP-ha7m), the commit door's lanes and the write boundary cover them, but the placed `dimension-discipline` and `ssot-doc-link` rules still fire only on the hardcoded `docs/**/*.md`, `README.md`, `plugins/*/README.md` in their `paths:` frontmatter. Authoring in a declared folder (e.g. `kinds/*.md`) gets no rule at the decision moment. Static YAML cannot read the declaration.
**Area:** `plugins/super-bootstrap/skills/harness-bootstrap/assets/rules-dimension-discipline-skeleton.md:5`, `rules-ssot-doc-link-skeleton.md:5`, `claude-md-skeleton.md:121,123`; harness-bootstrap's rule placement step
**Prior:** harness-bootstrap renders the declared globs into the placed rules' `paths:` on placement and re-run; the frozen-asset byte-identical refresh then needs a defined render step.

## Verdict — surface · 2026-11-09

### Findings

- premise age: 1 commit touched the named surfaces after the observation — `e51eb52` (GAP-ha7m's landing; it edits `claude-md-skeleton.md` § Doc Sync lines 74/80 only, not the § Rules bullets at 121/123 or either rule skeleton). The premise stands.
- root cause: confirmed against current code. `rules-dimension-discipline-skeleton.md:2-5` and `rules-ssot-doc-link-skeleton.md:2-5` hardcode `docs/**/*.md`, `README.md`, `plugins/*/README.md`. `harness-bootstrap/SKILL.md:239` places both "if missing (no substitutions)", and `SKILL.md:173` drift-checks their "whole shipped bodies" against the asset. So a consumer that hand-adds a declared glob to `paths:` gets that line flagged as drift on the next re-run and reverted on approval. No path makes a declared folder fire the rules today.
- card correction, glob semantics: the card's Prior assumes the declared globs render into `paths:` as they are. They do not share semantics. `.claude/doc-surface` matches `*` across `/` (`doc-links.sh:151-158`; `CLAUDE.md` § Doc Sync: "`*` crossing `/`"). `paths:` matches gitignore-style (`rules-index-skeleton.md:13`), where `*` stops at `/`. A verbatim render of `kinds/*.md` fires on `kinds/x.md` but misses `kinds/sub/y.md`, which the commit door's lanes do cover. Any render route needs a defined translation (e.g. `*` → `**` where it can cross a segment), with a test.
- interaction with open `GAP-naw8`: its last Amendment names the felt re-run cost as `claude-config-manager`'s audit chain, which gates any commit under `.claude/rules/` (`GAP-naw8.md:185-188`). A render route makes every `.claude/doc-surface` edit owe a re-run plus a harness audit, and the rules stay silently stale until that re-run. `GAP-naw8.md:203` also floats a route that relies on placed files being byte-identical to the skeleton. A rendered rule breaks that byte match.
- no closed fork re-walked: `docs/decisions.md` has no row on rule `paths:` rendering. Row 69 (`paths:` fires on a file read, not on intent) fits here, because a doc edit is a file-read moment.
- duplicate check: no other open card covers this. `GAP-naw8` overlaps on mechanism (re-run burden, hook delivery) but not on aim.
- scope reach: `harness-bootstrap/SKILL.md:173,239,336-337` (placement and drift contract), both rule skeletons' frontmatter, `claude-md-skeleton.md:121,123` and dogfood `CLAUDE.md` § Rules bullets (they narrate "fires on …"), `rules-index-skeleton.md:13` (glob-semantics note), and `.claude/rules/repo-boundary.md` § Sync direction (the asset lane's "byte-identical" refresh for rule skeleton bodies). The dogfood `.claude/rules/` copies stay byte-identical while this repo declares no `.claude/doc-surface`, which it does not today.
- attempted: static read only. No `§ Probes` table applies. I stopped at the design fork below. Test strategy is not the blocker: whichever route is picked, a render step or a hook is testable without human eyes (render: a unit test over the translation plus a cold dry-run of the SKILL.md step per `skill-authoring.md`; hook: a fixture test).

### Decision needed

- Which mechanism carries the doc rules onto the declared surface, given that the choice trades the harness-audit cost the author named on `GAP-naw8` against delivery risk?
- options:
  - (A) Render at placement (the card's Prior, corrected). On placement and re-run, harness-bootstrap appends the declared globs, translated to gitignore semantics, after the skeleton's three entries in both placed rules' `paths:`. The drift check compares against skeleton plus rendered tail, not the raw asset. The asset-lane contract becomes "byte-identical modulo the rendered `paths:` tail". Cost: the rules go stale between a declaration edit and the next re-run, with no signal. Each declaration change owes a `.claude/rules/` commit and so a harness audit. It breaks the byte match `GAP-naw8:203` relies on.
  - (B) Read the declaration live through a plugin hook. A plugin `PreToolUse`/`PostToolUse` hook on Read|Edit|Write matches the file against `.claude/doc-surface` at fire time and injects both rule bodies (or a pointer to them). The placed rules and their contract stay untouched. Nothing goes stale and nothing extra is audited. Risk: plugin-hook output reach is unverified on the current build (`GAP-naw8.md:159`), and the change adds a new hook surface to maintain beside the static `paths:` lane.
  - (C) Consumer tail, no render. The drift check treats `paths:` entries after the skeleton's three as consumer-owned (the way CLAUDE.md § Rules treats its tail). The consumer adds the translated globs by hand, and harness-bootstrap only checks for them and warns when `.claude/doc-surface` declares a glob no rule covers. This is the smallest change to the contract, but the consumer keeps three lists in sync.
- recommendation: (C), with (A)'s coverage warning folded in. It fixes the real blocker (the drift check reverting a consumer's own addition) and adds no render step or hook. It makes a stale gap visible on re-run, and it does not decide `GAP-naw8`'s open audit-cost and delivery question ahead of that card. Move up to (B) if `GAP-naw8`'s hook-reach probe passes.
- settles by: author preference on the harness-maintenance trade-off. The author named the audit chain as the felt cost (`GAP-naw8` Amendment), and `GAP-naw8` is held open ("這session不決定"). (B)'s delivery half also needs a `dispatched probe`: a positive-control token through a plugin `PostToolUse` hook on Read of a declared-surface file, read in the main session and in a subagent, on the current build.
