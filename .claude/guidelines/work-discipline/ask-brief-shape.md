# Ask Brief Shape — Six Slots in the Reader's Attention Order

A brief handed to a human spends the one attention the work cannot replace,
so it is ordered by what the reader needs first and not by how the case was
built. The first line says what is being decided; every slot after it exists
so the reader can stop early. A brief that opens on background buys a ruling
on the background instead of on the decision.

Six slots, in this order:

1. **What you're deciding** — one line, with the recommendation in it.
   Required.
2. **Why it's you** — the root that puts the call on the reader's side of
   the wall: only a human can judge it, or only a human can be accountable
   for it. Required: it lets the reader tell at a glance whether the ask
   should have reached them at all.
3. **Situation** — the problem or the concern, one or two sentences.
   Required. An item is not presumed to be a problem; there is always a
   situation.
4. **Premises** — what is already verified, each with its source, so the
   reader does not redo the investigation. Required.
5. **Impact** — what follows from taking the recommendation and from not
   taking it, per option wherever options exist. Required.
6. **Other options** — only where they exist, each differing from the
   recommendation in angle rather than in degree: a required options slot
   gets filled with near-identical choices, which is why this one is
   optional. Each option stands on its own, needing no context the brief
   left behind in the session.

Where the reader asked for the instruction alone — "just tell me which one"
— the brief is itself the attention waste the shape exists to prevent.
Answer the question.

## Boundary

Fires once an ask has survived the threshold and something is about to reach
a human for a ruling — a reply, a modal, a review page, one item inside a
batch — and governs what that one item contains. Whether the ask is owed,
and how far a batch may grow, belong to the ambient ask-threshold rule that
points here. Both sit under the **escalation-design** axiom — surface with
context rather than deviate silently — which also owns the two roots slot 2
names. Its three-part blocked surface is a different artifact from this one,
routed to a dispatcher who must unblock the work. The agent-facing
counterparts are
[`dispatch-brief-shape.md`](dispatch-brief-shape.md) and
[`dispatch-brief-demands.md`](dispatch-brief-demands.md) — a different
reader, a different firing moment, and a brief that answers to a container's
write set rather than to a person's attention. Why ordering by the reader
beats ordering by the argument is
[`attention-efficiency.md`](attention-efficiency.md).
