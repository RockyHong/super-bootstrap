# Attention Efficiency — The Goal "Simple" Is Serving

"Simple" names a *form* — shorter sentences, fewer sections, plainer words —
and a form is reachable while the reader's cost goes up, so the form is not
the target. The target one layer under it is the reader: instant
understandability, readability, the lowest cognitive friction between landing
on the material and using it. The target one layer above that is **attention
efficiency** — the same understanding, or the same action, reached with less
of the reader's attention. Where less of it will do, sharpen. Where less of
it will not, the shorter version is the more expensive one.

- **Name the reader before judging simple.** Simple is relative to who
  reads: a field term is one token to a practitioner and a definition lookup
  to everyone else, so the same sentence is the cheap version for one reader
  and the costly version for another. Until the intended reader is named,
  "simpler" has no direction and the work drifts toward whatever is easiest
  to write. Name them, then compose at their vocabulary, their priors, and
  what they already hold.
- **Read the cost off the reader, not off the text.** Word count, section
  count and reading level are proxies that move independently of what the
  reader spends. A cut that makes the reader re-derive what was cut moved the
  proxy and raised the cost. The readings that bind are whether the reader
  grasps it on first pass and can act on it without a second one.
- **Sharpen wherever less attention reaches the same place.** Attention
  efficiency is a direction, not a ceiling to stay under: one word carrying
  what a sentence carried, an ordering that removes a backtrack, one concrete
  case replacing a paragraph of abstraction, the answer sitting where the
  reader lands rather than after its derivation — each is owed once it is
  available, not merely allowed.
- **Keep the core model whole and plain the words around it.** This is the
  corollary that stops the rule above from eating the content: words plainer
  than the model they carry force the reader to rebuild the model, and
  rebuilding costs more attention than the plain words saved. The core model
  is not sacred — it holds its place by that arithmetic alone, and it goes
  the moment the reader reaches the same result without it.

## Boundary

Applies to material generated for a human reader and to how it is handed
over — replies, prose docs, reports, generated artifacts. This file owns the
**composition** decision: how delivered material is shaped for the reader it
is for. Which surface material lives on — the context-load versus
cognitive-load call — is the **placement** decision, owned by the
**attention-budget** axiom's two-budgets rule: same currency, different
decision, and placement runs first. Lines written for an agent to read
(skills, agents, rules, instruction files) carry their own shape rules under
the **harness-md-discipline** axiom, cut test included. Doc mechanics with
their own homes stay there — the retrieval breadcrumb (link each concept to
its SSOT home) and dimension purity (state versus history).
