# Operations

How an operator runs the brindle cabinet during a shift.

## Loading

Slide the trays in from the top shelf down and close the door. The panel shows the
recipe name and the target humidity before it starts.

## Power loss

After a power cut the cabinet starts a recovery ramp: it climbs back to the recipe's
set points at no more than 2 °C per minute, so the dough skin does not crack. The panel
shows the ramp's progress bar. Keep the door closed for the whole recovery ramp — an
open door restarts it from the current reading.

## Line backup

Press Pause when the oven line backs up. The cabinet drops into standby: the timer
stops while temperature and humidity stay at their set points. Press Resume to leave
standby and continue.

## Unloading

Open the door only after the panel chimes. Pull the trays from the bottom shelf up so
condensation does not drip onto finished dough.
