# Operations

How an operator runs the brindle cabinet during a shift.

## Loading

Slide the trays in from the top shelf down and close the door. The panel shows the
recipe name and the target humidity before it starts.

## Unloading

Open the door only after the panel chimes. The panel chimes once when a rise ends.
Pull the trays from the bottom shelf up so condensation does not drip onto finished dough.

## End of shift

The steps are in [End of shift](shutdown.md).
